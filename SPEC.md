\# Movie Watchlist – Spec



Movie Watchlist is a simple web app for keeping track of movies you want to watch. You make an account, add movies to your list, and once you watch one you can mark it as watched and give it a rating out of 5 stars.



Main data: Movie – title, release year, genre, status (to watch / watched), rating (1–5), notes

Pages: Login/Register, Watchlist (view, add, edit, delete)

Login needed: Yes – each user has their own private list, so nobody else can see or mess with your movies
